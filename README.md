# Từ Vựng
Pastel offline flashcard PWA. Ships with no words: load your own list (JSON or CSV front,back,hint,publishedAt) on first launch. Data stays in the browser.

## v10 review rules
- Due Practiced/Learned reviews come before new words or new grammar. New material uses only the daily goal allowance left after reviews; you can still finish overdue reviews after reaching the goal.
- Four unassisted correct reviews move a new word to Learned: after 1, 2, then 4 calendar days (about seven days minimum). Learned reviews start after 14 days and double, capped at 120 days.
- Kinda, a correct answer after Hint, near-spelling answers, and Show answer keep the current section/stage and schedule a retry tomorrow. They do not count as an unassisted success. A genuinely wrong answer or Didn't know resets the word and repeats it in the session.
- Vertical swipes never grade a card. Use the Kinda button; horizontal swipes still grade Didn't know / Knew it.
- A review is eligible all day on its due date, including legacy timestamp-based dates. No early spot-checks. The word list shows the stage and next review date.
- Existing local progress, word-list merge behavior, flashcard direction, grammar settings and goals are retained. No reset or reimport is needed to update. Restoring a progress backup replaces progress only after confirmation.
